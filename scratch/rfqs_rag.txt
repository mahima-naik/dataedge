# RFQ Knowledge Base

## Procucev Quotation Management Guide

### RFQ Process Overview
1. RFQ Submission
2. Requirement Clarification
3. Quotation Preparation
4. Quotation Delivery
5. Follow-up & Negotiation
6. Order Finalization

### Standard Information for Quotations
- Product/Service specifications
- Quantity required
- Delivery location
- Required delivery date
- Special requirements
- Budget constraints (if any)

### Pricing Components
- Unit cost
- Volume discounts (if applicable)
- Delivery charges
- Taxes and duties
- Any additional charges

### Delivery Options
- Standard delivery (15-30 days)
- Expedited delivery (5-7 days) - Premium charges apply
- Bulk shipments (customized timelines)
- Drop-shipping options available

### Payment Terms Offered
- 30 days Net
- 50% advance + 50% on delivery
- Letter of Credit (for large orders)
- Customized terms for corporate contracts

### Quality Assurance in Quotations
- All quoted items are certified
- Product specifications guaranteed
- Quality inspections included
- Warranty information included in quotation

### Common RFQ Scenarios
1. **New Vendor Request**
   - Provide reference quotations
   - Explain our advantages
   - Offer trial quantities at competitive rates

2. **Bulk Order RFQ**
   - Calculate volume discounts
   - Offer flexible delivery schedule
   - Negotiate payment terms

3. **Urgent RFQ**
   - Highlight expedited options
   - Show what can be done within timeline
   - Offer alternatives if original timeline not possible

4. **Specialized/Custom RFQ**
   - Clarify all requirements
   - Provide detailed engineering specifications
   - Timeline for customization

### Documentation in Quotation
- Quotation valid for 30 days
- Revision date clearly marked
- All terms and conditions specified
- Contact information for follow-up
- Order process clearly explained

### Follow-up Strategy
- Acknowledge receipt of RFQ immediately
- Provide quotation within agreed timeframe
- Follow up after quotation delivery
- Be prepared to negotiate if needed
- Keep records of all communications
